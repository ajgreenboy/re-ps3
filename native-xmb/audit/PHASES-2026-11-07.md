## 1. Where it stands

The native build is a working PS3 XMB front end with real content: local media with resume and subtitles, Jellyfin (browse,
direct play and HLS, quality selection, text subtitles, remembered sign-in), Spotify (PKCE sign-in, Connect control), DLNA
discovery, Steam (library, friends, downloads, account switching, play activity), RAWG artwork, playlists, and user
management. The renderer ports most of the firmware's shader paths (background, ribbon, particles, icons, text, screensaver
dim). The last 40 commits (2026-11-05 to 11-07) are mostly product work:
`d9e2af8` game artwork (RAWG matching, Artwork Settings, hero backgrounds), `aff3cb6` Steam play activity and actions,
`56bb107` native DLNA/UPnP client and pages, `22413f6` playlist Move Up/Down and Add All, `ce8a33e` Jellyfin streaming quality,
`44ee535` Spotify real-account fixes, `3b18708` playlists, `66e0f4b` Jellyfin server text subtitles,
`aeb2ae3` user management from explore_plugin, `a8d4e83`/`0dfeee3` screensaver traced and corrected. The rest are fidelity
traces: Options hint plate, gaiji from `imagefont.bin`, particle wind and dust, page-open traces, settings page sweeps.

What is not done, in three groups. (1) HTPC behaviour: "Turn Off the System" only quits the XMB (`src/main.cpp:1018-1027`,
`Shell::requestQuit` then `app.quit()`), so the Windows desktop is what the TV then shows; Copy and Delete are listed disabled
(`src/shell.cpp:2442-2445`, comment at `:2959`); the audio output device row is read-only (`src/host.cpp:1627`); nothing
exists for Tailscale (client written, not built), Wake-on-LAN, Home Assistant, weather or self-update (grep of `src/` for
`wake`, `magic packet`, `homeassistant`, `weather`, `cec`: no product hits at the time; Tailscale has since landed in `22369f9`, see H2). (2) Product gaps from
`FEATURE-STATUS-2026-11-04.md` that are still open after its own 11-07 updates: the two-pane playlist editor, avatars, Log Out
and Automatic Login, Photo Gallery, in-game XMB, dynamic themes, Jellyfin backdrop draw, image subtitles, real-server DLNA and
free-account Spotify verification. (3) Fidelity: layout constants are still measured rather than traced (`audit/LAYOUT-TRACE.md`),
several firmware surfaces are absent (`audit/FIDELITY-SURFACES-MAP.md`: Internet Browser, game boot splash S31, auto-off guide
S29, OSK variants S24, music full-screen page S19), and verification is repeatability, not firmware parity
(`audit/VERIFICATION-HARNESS.md`).

Updated 2026-11-07 (Q1 docs pass): the doc drift below is reconciled in the markdown (HANDOFF-PHASES.md next steps and item 2,

## 3. Parallel workstreams

Collision map: `shell.cpp` (4778 lines), `view.cpp` (4271), `shell.h` and `CMakeLists.txt` are the shared hot files. The
rule: one workstream owns each of them at a time. New-file workstreams deliver their registration as a short hunk list (as
`SESSION-POWER.md` did) for the owner to apply.

| Workstream | Items | Owns (writes) | Does not touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Network and home | H2, H6, H7, P5 (page only), P7 | new files only: `tailscale_shell.cpp`, `wol.*`, `wol_shell.cpp`, `ha_client.*`, `ha_shell.cpp`, `weather_*`, `update_check.*`; stubs in `C:\dev\out\native-<name>\` (outside the repo, per `SPOTIFY.md`); an audit file per feature | `shell.*`, `view.*`, `CMakeLists.txt` (hunks handed to workstream C) |
| B. System and input | H1, H8, H10, F2 (cue), P1 and P2 verification | `host.cpp/h`, `pad.cpp/h`, `ps_watcher.*`, `main.cpp`, `interaction_audio.*`, `settings_menu.cpp` | `shell.cpp`, `view.cpp` (hunks for C and D) |
| C. Shell owner (library and lifecycle) | H3, H9, H5 (shell side), P3, P4, Q1, registration of A's pages | `shell.cpp/h`, `profiles.*`, `jellyfin_*`, new `file_ops.*`, `CMakeLists.txt`, `app_editor.*` | `view.cpp` |
| D. View owner (draw and fidelity) | H4, F1, F9, F3 (draw), F6 (font/colour), F7 | `view.cpp/h`, `coldboot.cpp`, `p3t.*`, `text_renderer.*`, audit docs | `shell.cpp` (requests accessors from C) |

Order: A and B start immediately and in parallel (no conflicts). C and D run in parallel with each other (disjoint files).
A and B hand hunks to C when a feature is ready. Ranked start: H1 (B), H2 (A), H3 (C), H4 (D), then H6/H7 (A), H5 (C+B).
Do not run two agents against the build output directory at once: `build.ps1` fails with LNK1104 while the exe is running
(`HANDOFF-PHASES.md`, ground rules); use `-OutputDirectory ../out/native-<name>` per workstream.
