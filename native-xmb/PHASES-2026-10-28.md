# Implementation phases, 2026-10-28

Forward plan for the native C++/Qt6 4.93 XMB recreation. It replaces
[PHASES-2026-10-27.md](PHASES-2026-10-27.md) (product track, phases 1-7) and
[PARITY-PHASES.md](PARITY-PHASES.md) (fidelity track, P0-P11) as the plan of record, and keeps both
as dated records. The completion ledger in section 1 was audited against the tree at `6096dbf`; it now
carries the evidence of the wave that landed after it (through `a445f4a`, section 0.2). Sections 2-6 are
the work that is actually left.

Read with: [HANDOFF-2026-10-27.md](HANDOFF-2026-10-27.md) (tree, build, gate, ground rules),
[WORKSPACES.md](WORKSPACES.md) (ownership split, which these phases obey),
[audit/PARITY-SCORECARD.md](audit/PARITY-SCORECARD.md) (the fidelity instrument),
[audit/FIRMWARE-PARITY-GAPS.md](audit/FIRMWARE-PARITY-GAPS.md) (the gap register).

## 0. What changed, and the one risk that matters

Sixteen commits landed after the fidelity plan and eighteen after the product plan (HEAD `6096dbf`,
194 commits, 90 source files, about 24,100 lines, 56 audit documents). That is the plan's baseline. HEAD
is now `a445f4a` - 210 commits, 93 source files, 27,703 lines, 59 tracked audit documents - and section
0.2 records what landed between the two. The product track went from
plan to code: Phase 1 Jellyfin completion (`3e5bcdf`: resume seek fixed and clamped, Watched on
completion, posters through the authenticated image endpoint with a bounded tag-keyed cache, OSK
search, server audio/subtitle streams with honest disabling, opt-in DPAPI remembered sign-in);
Phase 2 materials and text (`23980a5`: the firmware glass icon program became the normal path
wherever an icon draws at full `_Ambiant.w` with the opaque material demoted to dimmed states,
ICONS.mnu traced to four clock keyframes, `elideToWidth` plus a firmware-shape ellipsis and caret
metrics wired at 0.11 px agreement, UI-layer glare measured then rejected); Phase 3's reader and
profile layer (`c11d8d1`: every column resolves from its XMBML definition in the CLI, 38/38
profile round-trip checks, profile-scoped preferences with a v1->v2 migration); Phase 4 session and
power (`067d6f0`: Auto-Off darkens the display instead of quitting, with a `SC_MONITORPOWER`
broadcast and a `GUID_CONSOLE_DISPLAY_STATE` read-back, kiosk mode verified frameless on a chosen
display, real DualSense/DS4 battery and transport parsing hardware-verified); Phase 6's service
data layer (`b339422`: Steam `+app_stop` argument shape and `steamVerifyStop`, a two-step
account-switch state machine with a dry run, an avatar pipeline with a real fetch, the trophy
verdict, and the unavailability wording); Phase 7's operations material. On the fidelity track, six
firmware recoveries with measured specs (dialogs `e4e3c23`, item menu and information panel
`969c9ac`, OSK plus settings plus root geometry `4bbff6e`, media panels `79f377d`), the runtime RCO
object model (`6096dbf`: 122 containers, 8,070 objects, 165 animations, zero parse failures), and a
workspace split (`7e14147`) whose per-workspace handoffs were then deliberately scratched
(`21758fd`). Five passes were uncommitted in the shared tree when this plan was written: the dialogs
implementation, phase 5.1's named-state expansion and column-aware routes, the P0 parity corpus,
`work/perf-harden.ts`, and a scene pass. All five have since landed (dialogs `e171263`/`6cf54a4`, phase
5.1 and perf `4c4b25e`, P0 `09ae471`/`df2a286`, scene `9545047`); what is in flight now is section 0.1.
The single biggest risk is not any of those: it is
that the fidelity track still has no matched reference - one registered frame that differs by
construction, 0 of 25 canonical cells with a Tier B number, 1 of 25 with a reference at all (Tier A is
19 of 25, Tier C 6 of 25) - so
every measured spec delivered this week (geometry constants, dialog layouts, the OSK grid, page
classes) has no falsifier, and the corpus that would supply one is rig-bound to one machine whose
console input config needs the user's approval. Until Phase 9 fills cells, "closer" is a judgement
rather than a measurement, and the only defence is that each phase below names the dump, capture or
scorecard that closes it.

## 1. Completion ledger

Status rules, applied strictly:

- **done** - the item as written is satisfied and a commit, a measured run or a document section
  proves it.
- **partly** - a real part landed; the "missing" column names the rest. No row is called done
  because its scaffolding exists.
- **not started** - nothing in `src/` or `work/` implements it; a design in an audit does not count.
- **obsolete** - the item should not be carried forward, with the reason.

## 6. What "done" would mean, and what it costs

Done is not "the XMB looks right". Done is: every screen the console can reach has Tier A dump
equality and a Tier B number inside a matched, dated budget, with Tier C for the screens that have a
transition; the irreducible list is written with a measured size per item instead of a paragraph; one
harness (per track) gates the result and a regression fails the release; and the documents state the
score rather than an impression. On the product side, done means the remaining 24 not-started items
and the 47 partial ones resolve into either a working surface with evidence or a written refusal the
UI shows - no third state.

The honest cost, counted in agent sessions by the workstreams above: Phase 8 about 3, Phase 9 about 6
to 9 (plus several days of serial rig time the agents cannot parallelise), Phase 10 about 4 to 5,
Phase 11 about 6 to 8, Phase 12 about 6 to 8, Phase 13 about 6 to 8, Phase 14 about 5 to 7, Phase 15
about 8 to 11, Phase 16 about 3 to 4. That is roughly **45 to 60 agent sessions**, and the wall clock
is not that number divided by eight: the ui workspace is the critical path (Phases 10, 12 and 13 all
need `view.cpp`, one writer), so about 20 sessions serialise behind that fence, and the rig is one
machine with a human at the input config. At the pace this project has actually run at, expect two to
four coordinated weeks for phases 8-14, with phase 15's real-service items gated on the user providing
a Steam title run and the profile UX decision. Phase 9 is the phase that must not slip: everything
after it can be re-planned, and nothing after it can be verified without it.
